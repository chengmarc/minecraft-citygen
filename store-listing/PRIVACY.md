MC CityGen Privacy Policy
Last updated: October 15, 2026

This policy covers MC CityGen everywhere it ships: the Microsoft Store edition, the Windows installer, the portable build, and running from source.

Summary

MC CityGen does not collect, store, send or share any personal information. It works entirely on your computer and never connects to the internet.

What the App Does With Data

• No network access. MC CityGen has no telemetry, analytics, crash reporting, advertising, accounts or update checks. It never sends data to the developer or to anyone else.
• Local files only. The app reads the MC world folders you choose. It writes its outputs (generated worlds, schematics, previews), your saved settings, and a startup error log if one is needed, all on your computer:
• Microsoft Store edition: %LOCALAPPDATA%\Packages\<package>\LocalCache\Local\MC CityGen\ (removed when you uninstall the app)
• Windows installer and portable build: the app's own folder, or %LOCALAPPDATA%\MC CityGen\ if that folder can't be written to
• Running from source: the source folder, or your user's application data folder
• No personal information. None of these files contain personal information beyond what you put into them yourself, such as folder paths or world names.

Third Parties

MC CityGen includes no third-party services that collect data. Microsoft may collect information about Store downloads and installs under the Microsoft Privacy Statement, https://privacy.microsoft.com/privacystatement, separately from MC CityGen.

Children

MC CityGen does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published with the app's next update, and the "Last updated" date will change.

Contact

Questions about this policy can go to the project's issue tracker: https://github.com/chengmarc/minecraft-citygen/issues
